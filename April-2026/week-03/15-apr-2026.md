Title: Marketing Website Credibility Remediation, Design System Formalisation, 25+ Page Redesign Sweep, and Typography System Rebuild

Objective:
To execute a comprehensive credibility and quality remediation of the marketing website by first synchronising all seven repositories across three branches, then conducting a deep audit to identify blocking defects, running a multi-wave Super Engineer Review Board to validate the remediation plan, redesigning 25+ pages through page-by-page interactive review against a newly commissioned 2026 design reference study, and culminating in a complete typography system rebuild that transforms the site from 22 distinct pixel values and 6 font weights into a unified 12-class scale with 4 weights, backed by a fluid clamp()-based scaling strategy and a shared MDX component map.

Work Carried Out:

**Ecosystem-Wide Branch Synchronisation and Pull Request Merge Train:** The session opened with a full alignment of the protocol ecosystem across all seven repositories and three branches. The eco-system branch was 45 commits behind dev and required a fast-forward followed by a reverse merge of 18 remote commits covering marketing, partner models, and as-built documentation, during which five submodule conflicts were resolved with dev branch priority. Seven pull requests were then created and merged from dev into main across the entire estate, covering 1,232 backend-core commits, 726 user-wallet commits, 264 command-centre commits, 50 website commits, 28 institutional admin commits, 8 blockchain commits, and 1,024 parent repository commits, bringing dev, eco-system, and main to identical commit state. An infrastructure defect was also corrected where the parent repository remote was incorrectly pointing to the blockchain submodule repository rather than the parent, and a corrupted blockchain submodule pointer introduced during the eco-system merge was reset to its correct reference.

**Deep Audit of the Marketing Website:** A comprehensive audit identified 15 discrete defects organised by severity: three critical issues including a newsletter link pointing to example.com, a missing video file reference, and the homepage plus three additional pages lacking metadata; three high-severity issues including console.log statements in production, placeholder text visible to participants, and API security configuration gaps; four medium-severity issues covering terminology, missing dynamic metadata, hardcoded colours, and duplicate footer entries; and five low-severity issues relating to test pages and demo components. The audit formed the foundation for the first review board cycle and established the baseline quality score for the remediation effort.

**Nine-Agent Super Engineer Review Board for the Credibility Plan:** The remediation plan was subjected to a three-wave review board. Wave 1 dispatched four parallel research agents covering codebase exploration, best practices investigation, infrastructure verification, and risk mapping, producing the discovery that seven of the 35 plan assumptions were incorrect including a pre-existing generateMetadata implementation and a foundingDate conflict between 2024 and 2023-10. Wave 2 dispatched five reviewers in parallel as chief architect, testing lead, security lead, DevOps lead, and quality lead, returning conditional approval with a 7.2/10 quality score, specific findings on a whitepaper page requiring a layout metadata wrapper due to its "use client" directive, and instructions to gate tsc --noEmit checks at every stage. Wave 3 deduplicated 28 findings and refined the plan from v1 to v2 with a projected quality score of 8.4. Implementation proceeded through four waves with build gates passing at every stage.

**Brand-Consistent Image Generation and Skill Codification:** Eleven brand-aligned images were generated through fal.ai using the flux-pro/v1.1 endpoint, covering four card images for the seed network, marketplace exchange, economic engine, and community covenant sections, four community circle images representing pioneers, builders, merchants, and stewards, a craftsperson hands hero background, a local market aerial view for the community section, and an OG social share image. A permanent /image-generation skill was codified at .claude/skills/image-generation/SKILL.md capturing deep brand understanding so future image generation runs can reproduce the aesthetic without rediscovering it.

**Seven-Agent UI/UX Consistency Review Board and Design System Foundation:** A second review board was convened specifically for visual consistency, surfacing findings including 27 instances of excessive py-20 lg:py-40 spacing across 18 files, a SectionHeading component that was used in four of eight homepage sections but zero of six sub-pages, missing gradient orbs across all credibility pages, and a noise.png texture file that was 0 bytes. Phase 0 of the design system foundation followed, producing a GradientOrbs shared component with four variants, a GlassCard shared component with responsive padding at p-6/sm:p-8/md:p-10, a Section wrapper component combining spacing, background, orbs, and max-width, a four-tier spacing scale added to globals.css covering compact, standard, major, and hero tiers, and a regenerated noise texture file. Five parallel agents then executed the protocol page visual upgrade across how-it-works, what-is-gxcoin, and economic-model, followed by the global spacing and credibility polish sweep.

**Homepage Gold Standard Upgrade and Accessibility Remediation:** The homepage was held up against the newly commissioned design reference and scored 6.3/10 by the UX Critic agent and 20+ violations by the Code Auditor. A critical accessibility bug was identified where aria-hidden="true" was hiding navigable Link elements in the audience card section from screen readers, rendering the entire For Individuals, For Businesses, and For Governments cohort invisible to assistive technology. Three parallel agents then executed the homepage gold standard upgrade, with Agent 1 handling page.tsx and layout.tsx for the accessibility fix, SectionHeading insertion, accent bars, and metadata em dash cleanup, Agent 2 reworking the HeroSection by renaming brand-jargon CTAs "Genesis Moment" and "Comparative Analysis" into participant-friendly "Claim Your Allocation" and "How It Works" with proper href routing, and Agent 3 fixing all remaining components including community mobile grid collapse from 6-col to 3-col, emoji accessibility remediation in Technology.tsx, non-breaking hyphen insertion, dead JoinCTA button repair, ProtocolSection accent bar addition, and removal of two unused Google Font imports for performance.

**2026 Design Reference Study and Reality Check:** A 955-line design research document was produced by an Opus agent with extensive WebSearch research into award-winning sites, codified at docs/DESIGN-REFERENCE-2026.md as a permanent design bible. Key findings were disorienting: gradient orbs on every section is explicitly flagged as an anti-pattern because universal decoration dilutes brand impact, dark-first design is the 2026 standard with over 80% of mobile users having dark mode enabled, glassmorphism has matured into a tool rather than a style and should not be applied to every card, bento grids have replaced standard card grids on 67% of top SaaS/fintech sites, and CSS Scroll-Timeline is now fully supported since February 2026 allowing zero-JS reveal animations. When the homepage was scored against this reference the honest grade came back as 4.5/10, triggering a decision to execute eight selected recommendations while deferring dark mode and preserving the brand green colour against the reviewer's recommendation.

**Page-by-Page Redesign of Protocol Sub-Pages:** Rather than continuing with batch agent dispatches, a new working pattern emerged where each page was redesigned interactively with live review at localhost:4545. The /the-protocol parent page was standardised with consistent container widths after a 384-pixel jump between max-w-7xl and max-w-6xl was corrected, responsive padding was normalised across all five components, a generated watchmaker hero image was added, and a to-grey typo in PromisesSection was fixed. The what-is-gxcoin page was rebuilt with a stats banner of four key metrics, full-bleed alternating image and content panels for the three core principles, and audience cards with coloured top borders. The how-it-works page introduced a numbered step overview with large faded 01/02/03 indicators, alternating panels for Steps 1 and 2, and icon cards on zinc-50 for Step 3. The economic-model page was rebuilt with a 1.25 Trillion big-number centrepiece, four key metric cards, horizontal percentage allocation cards, split participant phase layouts, and a dark growth section.

**Economic Model Data Correction Against Source of Truth:** During the economic model redesign a significant data mismatch was identified where the page displayed a 1 Trillion total supply against every marketing document and the as-built specification stating 1.25 Trillion. The source of truth at infra-topol/as-built/09-TOKENOMICS-PARAMETERS.md last updated 2026-04-12 showed 1.25T total supply, five allocation pools rather than six after the Stewardship and Network Operations pools were merged into Protocol Operations, completely different percentages at 48.80% participant distribution versus the old 45.5%, and different absolute values at 610B versus 455B for participants. All page data was corrected verbatim to match the specification. This established a permanent rule that financial data must never be independently calculated and must always be verified against the tokenomics parameters document.

**For-Builders Section Rebuild and Partner Business Model Pivot:** The for-builders ecosystem received the most substantive content restructuring of the session. The validator page was rebuilt against SPEC-Validator-Operations.md after discovering that the existing page described the Expression of Interest fee as non-refundable when it is actually partially refundable with GX 200 returned on rejection, completely omitted the 5% transaction fee share plus 5% API revenue share model, used vague "enterprise-grade" hardware descriptions instead of the actual 16-core, 64GB DDR5, 8TB NVMe, three-server cluster specifications, and missed the Year 1 to Year 20 scaling plan from 20 to 800 validators. The explore-apis page went through four iterations: V1 exposed internal endpoint paths and service names, V2 was audited against actual route files discovering three non-existent endpoints and a svc-governance service that is only a stub with 10 source files, V3 was redesigned as a capability overview, and V4 pivoted entirely to showcase the partner business model with six partner program cards covering FSP, PMS, INS, MKT, CRD, VAL categories sourced from the Partner Ecosystem Framework v3. Charter tier license counts were also corrected from 100/400/1000/2500 to the spec values of up to 85 for Global/Continental/Regional and 10 per category per country for National tier.

**Community, About, and Content Page Sweep:** The community page received the standard sub-page treatment including hero background, full-bleed alternating panels, gradient section headings, and standardised rounded-2xl card corners, while non-functional buttons pointing to features that did not exist were removed in favour of informational-only cards. The forum page was rebuilt as a purposeful "Coming Soon" showing the vision for six planned sections, four community principles, and an early access CTA; during this redesign a no-emoji rule was established after Manazir flagged emoji icons with "Don't ever use emojis. The learn section had its five MDX articles completely rewritten to the whitepaper formatting standard, producing what-is-demurrage-currency.mdx at 378 lines with six numbered sections, productive-economy-explained.mdx at 320 lines, interest-free-currency-explained.mdx at 332 lines, non-speculative-cryptocurrency.mdx at 276 lines, and gx-coin-vs-other-gx-tokens.mdx at 191 lines.

**Twelve-Agent Typography Audit and Three-Wave Review Board:** After all 25+ pages were up to the design system standard, a comprehensive typography audit was commissioned that dispatched 12 specialised agents covering font size audit, component audit, gx-* adoption rate, line-height audit, font-weight overrides, remaining em dashes, and research into Stripe, Vercel, Linear, modular scale theory, small text standards, and 2025-2026 best practices. The audit surfaced 425 raw Tailwind text-* instances across 52 files against only 189 gx-* class usages for a 31% adoption rate, a line-height inheritance bug where gx-h1, gx-h2, and gx-h3 were inheriting the body line-height of 1.6 creating 77-pixel line-heights on 48-pixel headings, 77 !important font-weight overrides, 106 remaining em dashes predominantly in a single MDX file with 76 instances, and dead classes gx-h4, gx-lead-sm, and gx-subtle with zero usages alongside a gx-lead-2 class referenced 28 times but never defined. A three-wave review board followed: the Chief Architect approved with modifications flagging gx-p and gx-p-2 base properties as frozen and hero leading-tight overrides as intentional, the Quality Lead scored the plan 6/10 and caught that gx-font-gradient-light which was slated for deletion actually had one active usage in HeroSection.tsx which would have broken the homepage hero, and the Testing Lead contributed a risk matrix mapping each change to its blast radius and verification strategy.

**Typography System Rebuild Across Five Phases:** The approved plan v5 was then executed through five phases with build gates passing after each. Phase 0 added five new CSS classes gx-xs, gx-caption, gx-sm, gx-label, and gx-h4, and created the shared MDX component map at src/components/mdx/mdx-components.tsx applying gx-* classes to markdown-generated elements via React-level overrides rather than CSS prose classes. Phase 1a redefined sizes with fixed 16-pixel body text and fluid clamp() headings via CSS custom properties, correcting line-heights across the board with gx-p moving from 1.4 to 1.6 and gx-display from 1.05 to 1.1. Phase 1b standardised weights after a dark section readability audit identified 15 instances across 13 files where gx-p text on dark backgrounds needed proactive font-medium overrides before the weight change, then shifted gx-p from font-medium to font-normal, gx-p-2 from font-light to font-normal, and gx-display-1 from font-black to font-extrabold. Phase 2 wired the MDX component map into all three MDXRemote rendering paths for docs, learn, and blog slugs, removing all prose CSS classes from six files. Phase 3 dispatched three parallel agents that absorbed raw text instances with Agent 1 migrating 98 instances across 22 pages, Agent 2 migrating 63 instances across 22 components, and Agent 3 replacing 28 gx-lead-2 references in four MDX files. Phase 4 created the authoritative docs/TYPOGRAPHY.md specification, updated CLAUDE.md, and optimised the Google Fonts import from nine Inter weights down to four.

**Site-Wide Em and En Dash Elimination:** Throughout the session a firm rule was established that em and en dashes would never be used, with alternative punctuation of commas, colons, periods, semicolons, or pipes in metadata titles substituted instead. Three parallel background agents executed the final sweep, with Agent 1 fixing 23 em dashes across eight files plus five en dashes across five additional files in TypeScript code, Agent 2 fixing 78 em dashes across three MDX files including 74 in the gx-coin-api-suite.mdx file alone, and Agent 3 adding breadcrumb navigation to the five pages for-businesses, for-governments, for-individuals, comparative-analysis, and genesis-moment. The final verification grep confirmed zero em or en dashes remaining in any TSX, TS, or MDX file site-wide.

**Session Rule Codification and Design System Documentation:** Nine firm design and engineering rules were codified through real-time feedback throughout the session: no em or en dashes ever, no emojis in any content with SVG icons or nothing substituted, gradient applied to every gx-display-2 heading with the last significant words wrapped in gx-font-gradient, all financial data verified against the tokenomics parameters source of truth, rounded-2xl as the card corner standard, docs and learn slug pages use breadcrumbs while sub-pages use SubPageNavBar, sub-pages must have distinct layouts from parents and siblings with no mini-clones, gx-p and gx-p-2 base properties are frozen, and hero leading-tight overrides are intentional and preserved. Both the-website CLAUDE.md and the frontend skill were rewritten to document the complete design system including spacing tiers, shared components, gradient orb requirements, typography classes, and sub-page architecture patterns.
