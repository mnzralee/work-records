Title: Seven-Session Sunday Sprint: Pre-Registration Funnel Repair, 143-Screen Wireframe Marathon, Admin Console Scaffold & Website Content Parity

Objective:
To execute a multi-track Sunday sprint that simultaneously closed a silent funnel-leak defect on the public marketing site, advanced the protocol's participant-facing wireframe library through three full design phases plus a fourth kickoff, scaffolded an enterprise admin console behind a board-meeting-style specification, and drove three previously authored content-parity specifications through a six-phase execution against live data. The secondary goal was to enforce a review-board discipline across every track so that silent failures, canonical-source drift, role-drift across narrative wireframes, and missing test harnesses were surfaced loudly and resolved before reaching production. The tertiary goal was to land the work as a disciplined commit history, with file-by-file granularity and storytelling work records appended in real time, while keeping production regressions at zero.

Work Carried Out:

**Diagnosing a Silent Funnel Leak in the Pre-Registration Confirmation Flow:** The opening session targeted a defect on the public marketing site where the pre-registration form rendered a "You're On The List!" success panel the moment the API returned, even though the underlying flow only persisted the user to the Google Sheets waitlist after a confirmation email had been clicked and the token validated by a separate confirm endpoint. The success panel was therefore lying to every user whose email landed in spam or who never returned to their inbox, producing a silent funnel leak that no log line surfaced. A full trace of the submission path was drawn from the form component through the contact API into the Resend dispatch and out to the confirm endpoint, isolating the trust boundary at the token-verification step and identifying seven distinct UX gaps including the misleading success title, the absent confirmation cue, the missing spam-folder callout, the unconfirmed email echo, the absent seventy-two-hour expiry notice, the contact-form voice in the email template, and the generic confirm-page copy.

**Three-Layer Repair of the Pre-Registration UX:** Rather than collapsing the multi-step token flow into a single write, the fix preserved the security model and corrected the surface that had drifted from it. A `requiresEmailConfirmation` boolean and a `confirmationExpiresInHours` field were added to the form client configuration so future forms could opt into the confirmation pattern cleanly. The reusable form component was rewritten to render a "check your email" panel that echoed the submitted address, presented a three-step checklist, surfaced the seventy-two-hour expiry, and offered a "Submit again" reset path. A pre-registration-specific branded email template was added with waitlist-appropriate voice and dispatched conditionally on form identity. The confirm API was updated to return the form identifier so the success page could render per-form copy, swapping the pre-registration success state to "You're on the waitlist" with explore-protocol and return-home call-to-action buttons. A Playwright smoke harness exercised the full path and returned ten of ten green, with six post-audit polish fixes applied and committed as eight discrete website commits and one parent-pointer bump.

**Wireframe Phase 1 Marathon Across Nine Milestones:** A parallel design track drove the participant wallet wireframe through nine milestones in a single arc, opening with an orchestrator dispatch that staged the deliverables, working through canonical-source resolution against the protocol tokenomics specification, applying a Pass-2 canonical-values sweep, closing partial flows, splitting the basic-registration flow from the KYC onboarding flow after discovering they had been incorrectly fused, and running successive polish and flow-verification passes. A review board scored an early draft at seven out of ten with a four out of ten on protocol authenticity, prompting eight separate corrections including a user-productive reorientation, a backend-leads-wireframe inversion, a binary-KYC enforcement, the registration-versus-onboarding split, and a wallet-operations-versus-admin-operations separation. The Phase 1 file closed at sixty-five screens across thirteen sections, ten primitives plus three helpers, ten key insights captured, seventeen architectural decisions logged, six living documents produced, and seven docs-repository commits applied.

**Admin Console Specification, Scaffolding, and Multi-Agent Orchestration:** A separate parallel track convened a board-meeting-style design review for the admin console that will back the marketing data plane, producing SPEC-ADMIN-CONSOLE-V1 at six hundred lines across fifteen sections after a clarifying-questions pass and a safety assessment. A pre-flight pass discovered the production environment carried two useful surprises that informed the scaffolding pattern, and the implementation began with half-one of the foundation work covering the data plane and the Prisma schema. A pivot mid-track aligned the admin console operational pattern to the existing marketing site's pattern after a reminder that operational principles fail silently when imposed from outside. Multi-agent orchestration was then dispatched in two waves: the first wave landed Phase 2 dual-write scaffolding and Phase 3 authentication primitives across eight modules with seven API routes and middleware, returning fourteen commits; the second wave dispatched Phase 3 routes and Phase 4 shell in parallel, returning twenty-six additional commits. Two contract patches reconciled aggregation seams between the parallel tracks. The final track closed two previously-unnoticed CI failures: a Redis module-load throw that was resolved with a Proxy-based lazy-initialisation wrapper, and a Prisma types-missing failure inside the Docker build that was resolved by copying the schema into the dependency layer and regenerating in the builder stage. CI returned green and the pipeline auto-deployed the inert code to the staging environment pending runtime environment variables, closing at fifty-two website commits and thirteen parent commits across the session.

**Wireframe Phase 2 and Phase 3 Build-and-Polish Cycles:** The wireframe marathon continued into the institute wallet and the government wallet, each following the same discipline: a three-explorer research pass running in parallel against chaincode and the canonical specifications, a draft plan, a review-board cycle that surfaced canonical-correctness and user-experience findings, a build pass, and a three-reviewer polish pass with Playwright verification. Phase 2 closed at thirty-seven screens across six sections plus cover and reviewer notes, introducing eight new primitives including an organisation status pill, a signatory-rule band, an approval tally, a scoped context banner, and a transfer receipt; the review board caught a `velocityTaxExempt` assumption error that would have rendered an inaccurate institutional tax model. Phase 3 closed at forty-one screens across ten sections, introducing a further eight primitives including a treasury status pill, a phase-aware three-eyes tally with terminal-state overlays for return-arrows, expiry clocks, and cancellation strikes, a drawing-rights meter, a country treasury badge, and an official role matrix. The Phase 3 review board surfaced a live contradiction between the chaincode struct default and the protocol specification on the `velocityTaxExempt` flag, which was carried forward as engineering follow-up while the wireframe held to the specification value with a latent override variant.

**Phase 3 Narrative Coherence and the Audit Target:** The Phase 3 polish pass introduced a new failure mode that had not surfaced in earlier phases. With one treasury, ten role types, ten-level account hierarchies, and three-eyes flows rendered across forty-one screens, the demonstration narrative had to hold across multiple participant identities playing distinct roles. The user-experience polish reviewer read the file as one continuous story rather than a collection of screens and surfaced role-drift defects: a participant listed as approver on the official roster was initiating transfers everywhere else in the file, an authoriser-queue screen showed the wrong approver name relative to the rest of the narrative arc, and a treasury status pill had been misused on a loan proposal where a neutral annotation was the canonically accurate component. Two single-character canonical-language fixes were also applied, replacing a "citizens" reference with "participants" and rewording an error title that read as a rejection state despite the three-eyes protocol having no rejection path. Eleven fixes resolved the high-priority findings and Playwright re-verification returned zero console errors and zero React overlay artifacts.

**Phase 4 Kickoff and the Plan-Lock Discipline:** The fourth wireframe phase opened the admin console design surface where business, non-profit, and government participant types meet in a single scoped shell entered through an OAuth2 PKCE handoff carrying a thirty-minute scoped JWT. A three-explorer research pass mapped the existing twenty-two-route admin codebase, the canonical specifications for the admin console mission and type variants, and the backend signatory-rule simulation logic. A combined review board returned eleven priority-zero blockers, thirteen priority-one fixes, and ten priority-two polish items, each producing structural plan changes including the introduction of a distinct non-profit dashboard variant, a section split between business stakeholder management and government official management, a type-branched rule creation form, phase tabs on the government three-eyes queue, role-aware approve and authorise detail screens, type-enumerated compliance pills driven by country-business runtime data, and primitive type-guards preventing the three-eyes tally from rendering in business contexts and the stakeholder permission matrix from rendering in government contexts. The plan locked at thirty-four screens across eight sections with five new primitives specified including an institution-type pill, a rule-builder composite, a rule simulator with priority-ladder visualisation, a compliance pill, and an audit-diff component. The build itself was deferred deliberately to the next session, closing the kickoff with a clean carry-forward rather than a half-finished file.

**Website Content Parity Through a Six-Phase Super Engineer Review Board:** A late-day track executed three previously authored content-parity specifications through six phases. The initial plan was rejected after attempting to bypass the standing review-board rule on orchestration-layer plans, prompting a nine-reviewer three-wave Super Engineer Review Board that returned eighteen amendments across three critical, eight high, and seven medium severities. Phase zero installed a Playwright plus axe-core test harness as the prerequisite before any content edits landed. Phase A0 extracted three reusable primitives, generalised a world-map component that had been duplicating three hundred and eighty lines of country-rendering logic, and resolved a security finding while in the area. Phase A.1 ran a mechanical hygiene pass across links, citations, and fifteen blog images. Phase B drove the global-impact dashboard to full content parity against the published marketing analysis. Phase C drove the engagement-portal dashboard to partial parity within the day's wall-clock and finished early on the following day. The board pattern caught a staging-equals-production reality on the development branch, the absence of any functional test harness, a country-detail-modal component approaching four hundred lines that would have entered production without the primitive extraction, and a server-component boundary discipline drift that would have leaked client-only code into static rendering paths.

**Commit Discipline, Insight Capture, and the Day's Unifying Pattern:** The day closed at one hundred and forty-five commits across the parent repository and the documentation submodule on the day itself, with a further fourteen commits flowing into the next morning for the engagement-portal phase completion and three storytelling work-record append commits, totalling one hundred and fifty-nine commits. Three production deploys landed without regressions: the marketing site through continuous integration on every development-branch push, and the admin console code live but inert in staging pending runtime configuration. Thirty-one key insights were recorded across the seven sessions and three memory entries were persisted including a storytelling work-records discipline, a commit-discipline rule, and a foundation naming ruling. The day's unifying pattern across all seven sessions was the deliberate conversion of silent failure modes into loud ones: a form silently dropping users, a wireframe silently drifting from canonical specification values, an operational pattern silently violating the existing model, continuous integration silently red while work continued, and a development-branch push silently deploying to the production marketing domain. The review-board discipline earned its keep repeatedly, surfacing a critical maintenance-password literal in source control, the staging-equals-production reality, the absent test harness, the four-hundred-line component duplication risk, the server-component boundary drift, the velocity-tax-exemption assumption errors in two separate wireframe phases, the chaincode-versus-specification contradiction, and the basic-registration-versus-KYC flow fusion, each catch costing orders of magnitude less than the production debugging it averted.
