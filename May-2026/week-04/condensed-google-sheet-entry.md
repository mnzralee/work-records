A blue-green emergency restore brought the marketing site back through a parallel green container started on an unused port, health-checked in isolation, and promoted via the validated nginx upstream wrapper, with three deploy-path hardenings pinning the container HOSTNAME, joining the data-plane network with a fail-loud preflight, and gating health probes on the dependency-aware endpoint; a two-line session.ts fix corrected the cookie path with the legacy-clear path updated and the regressing unit test flipped to assert the requirement.
